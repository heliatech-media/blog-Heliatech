# The Quantum Leap of Artificial Intelligence

The artificial intelligence we know today, with its massive large language models (LLMs) and deep neural networks, relies on classical computing architecture. As powerful as they are, our supercomputers, [currently limited by traditional GPU architectures](/articles-en/16-hardware), will eventually hit a physical limit. This is where quantum computing enters the stage.

## What is Quantum Machine Learning (QML)?

Where a classical bit is either a 0 or a 1, a qubit can be both simultaneously thanks to the principle of superposition. By combining machine learning with quantum computing, we get QML. This synergy allows for processing vast amounts of data and exploring combinations of variables at speeds that even the most advanced supercomputers would take millennia to achieve.

## The End of Classical AI?

Quantum AI probably won't replace your usual voice assistant anytime soon. However, for fields requiring complex calculations, its impact will be revolutionary. Discovering new drugs through molecular simulation, optimizing global supply chains, or accurate climate modeling: quantum AI promises to solve the unsolvable.

Faced with these capabilities, current AI models will look as rudimentary as old calculators. The race for QML is already on between tech giants and nations, all aware that whoever masters this technology will hold the key to the next industrial era. The future is no longer just artificial; it is quantum.
